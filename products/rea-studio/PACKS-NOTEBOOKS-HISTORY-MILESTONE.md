## Backup pack ownership and integrity

[PACKS.md](apps/backup/PACKS.md) specifies data-only aggregation into packs bounded at 16 MiB, with at most 4,096 records and 8 MiB per plaintext blob. Tree records remain separate. Committed data IDs are deduplicated; the index refers to each accepted encrypted record by authenticated offsets and lengths.

Concurrent operations on the same writer reject with BUSY before inspection or mutation. Ownership remains held through flush, commit and cleanup, preventing rejected overlapping work from clearing previously accepted pending rows. The stage retains an independently reproduced pre-guard loss case and a guarded retry. This does not establish all cross-process or original locking semantics, complete key/plaintext zeroing, peak memory bounds or comparative speed.

The first current integration had 115 passing groups and one failed corruption-fixture assertion: its historical fixture assumed one blob per pack. That exact [failure collection](apps/backup/results/pack-aggregation-followup/failed-116/preservation-manifest.json) remains preserved. The corrected test authenticates every indexed record, mutates one ciphertext, preserves the other records byte-identically and recomputes offsets before asserting refusal. A later 64/64 behavior run completed successfully, but the parent collector failed parsing an escaped TAP diagnostic. Its [raw collection](apps/backup/results/pack-aggregation-followup/collector-failed-64/preservation-manifest.json) remains intact. Recovery parsed those existing results without rerunning behavior; a selected-test-list metadata correction and the previous receipt are retained separately.
