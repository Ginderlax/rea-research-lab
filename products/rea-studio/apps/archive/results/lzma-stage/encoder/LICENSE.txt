Source-assisted research implementation

Igor Pavlov's LZMA SDK 26.04 LzmaEnc.c, Lzma2Enc.c, LzmaDec.c and
Lzma2Dec.c are marked Public domain in the preserved source headers.
The SDK's public-domain statement is retained in reference/lzma-sdk.txt.

The new range/length/distance encoder structure was studied in, and derives
from, the pinned MIT lzma-purejs Encoder.js. The copied decoder derives from
the pinned MIT Juan Mellado Decoder.js. Neither is claimed as clean-room work.
